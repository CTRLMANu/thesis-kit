# My sources

Every paper, book or other source for the thesis. Each one has a status:

- **unchecked**: a lead. The AI never cites it in your text.
- **checked**: you opened it and wrote one line about it in your own words. Only these are cited.
- **dropped**: you looked at it and decided against it (it stays here so it isn't added again).

To add sources, paste them into the chat, or save PDFs into `papers/`, and say "add these sources". The AI looks each one up in the registries and adds it here. When you have read one, say "I read Müller 2021: it found …".

To cite a source in your text, write its **Cite as**, e.g. `[@muller2021]`; or write the citation normally and ask the AI to link it.
