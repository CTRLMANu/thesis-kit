# Start here

Your path from topic to hand-in. **Every step is optional**: skip one, change the order, or use a different tool ("I use Scite instead of Consensus"). The AI ticks steps off as you go. Ask **"what now?"** whenever you're unsure.

- [x] **1. Set up**: done.
- [ ] **2. Sharpen your question.** Turn your topic into one clear research question. Say: *"help me sharpen my question"*.
- [ ] **3. Make your exposé.** A first map of your field: the main research groups, where they disagree, the gaps, a first outline, and a list of papers worth opening. Say: *"make my exposé"*.
- [ ] **4. Search further.** Ask your question in Consensus, search Semantic Scholar, and let ResearchRabbit find papers connected to your best ones. Paste whatever you find into the chat and say: *"add these sources"*.
- [ ] **5. Open and check papers.** Open each paper that looks relevant. Keep the PDF in Zotero, and save a copy into `thesis/papers/` so the AI can read it too. When you've read one, say: *"I read Müller 2021: it found …"*. Only papers you checked this way are used in your text.
- [ ] **6. Read deeply.** Upload your PDFs to Gemini Notebook and ask them questions ("where do these papers disagree?"). Paste the useful answers here: *"file these notes"*.
- [ ] **7. Agree the outline with your supervisor.** Say: *"show me my outline"* and change it together until it matches what your supervisor expects.
- [ ] **8. Draft section by section.** Say: *"draft 2.1"*. The AI writes one section from your checked papers, and you rewrite it in your own words. Test any claim: *"check this sentence: …"*.
- [ ] **9. Move to Word.** Say: *"export to Word"*, then save the file into `thesis/word/` and keep working there. After each editing session, say: *"sync my Word file"*.
- [ ] **10. Work through feedback.** Paste your supervisor's comments, or sync a Word file that has them: *"make a checklist from these comments"*.
- [ ] **11. Hand in.** Say: *"write my AI declaration"*, then do a final *"export to Word"*.

## Research tools

All free to start, all optional. Use what you like; the kit accepts papers and notes from any tool.

| Tool | Use it to | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Consensus | ask your question in plain words and see what studies say | https://consensus.app |
| Semantic Scholar | search papers, with short summaries | https://www.semanticscholar.org |
| ResearchRabbit | find papers connected to the ones you like | https://www.researchrabbit.ai |
| Zotero | keep your papers and PDFs in one library | https://www.zotero.org |
| Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) | ask questions answered only from your own PDFs | https://notebooklm.google.com |

Also useful if you already use it: Elicit (https://elicit.com), which compares many studies in a table.
