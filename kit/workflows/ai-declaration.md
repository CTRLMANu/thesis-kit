# AI declaration

Drafts the declaration of AI use that many universities require, based on the AI log, for the student to check, correct and sign.

## Steps

1. Read `thesis/ai-log.md`; the university's AI rules and the thesis language in `thesis/profile.md`; the `Found via:` lines in `thesis/sources.md`; and the descriptions of saved versions (`git log --format='%ad %s' --date=short -- thesis`), which cover any period when the log was off.
2. Ask whether the university prescribes a form, table or wording. It is often part of the declaration of independent work (in German: *Eigenständigkeitserklärung*) or a list of aids used (*Hilfsmittelverzeichnis*). If the student pastes it, follow it exactly.
3. Draft, in the thesis language:
   - **Tools**: this kit and the AI model used in their editor (ask which if you can't tell), plus every research tool named in the log's `Outside tools` column or in a `Found via:` line.
   - **Purposes**, grouped: finding and checking literature; structuring (exposé, outline); first drafts of named sections that the student then rewrote; wording suggestions; checking claims against sources; formatting references.
   - **Where**: the sections where AI drafts were used, taken from the log.
   - **Responsibility**: a sentence saying that the student read and checked every cited source and revised all AI-drafted text, written for the student to confirm rather than asserted by you.
4. List separately anything the log doesn't cover (gaps, periods with the log off) and every statement the student must verify.
5. Save the draft as `thesis/ai-declaration.md` and ask the student to read every line and correct anything inaccurate.

## Done when

Every row of the AI log is reflected in the declaration or listed as left out with a reason, and the student has been asked to review it.
