# What now?

Tells the student where they stand and the single most useful next step. Also answers "what can you do?".

## Steps

1. Read `thesis/START-HERE.md`; the deadline in `thesis/profile.md`; `thesis/question.md`; `thesis/outline.md`; the status counts in `thesis/sources.md` (checked, unchecked, dropped); the `[CHECK: …]` and AI draft markers in `thesis/chapters/` and `thesis/drafts/`; and unticked items in `thesis/feedback/`.
2. Tick any step in `START-HERE.md` that the files show is already done.
3. Answer in at most three short paragraphs:
   - **Where you are**: one or two sentences with real numbers from the files ("14 papers, 5 checked; the outline has 5 chapters; 2.1 is drafted and still marked as an AI draft").
   - **Next step**: one concrete action, where to do it (a website, or here), and the exact words to say.
   - **Also useful** (optional): one or two alternatives, for example when the deadline is close, or something blocks the obvious step (the section they want to draft has no checked papers yet).
4. If they asked what you can do, list the jobs in plain words with one example sentence each, grouped as: finding papers, reading, writing, Word and supervisor, hand-in, and looking after the kit (update, backup, undo, AI log on or off).

## Done when

The reply names exactly one next step with the words to say or the site to open, and every number in "where you are" comes from the files.
